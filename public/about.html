About Hakku AI

PROACTIVE · ZERO-HALLUCINATION · ZERO-BRIBE CITIZEN OS

1. The Mission: Eliminating Welfare Leakage

Across India, governments allocate hundreds of thousands of crores to welfare programs every year. Yet, over 40% of eligible citizens—especially small farmers, students, construction workers, and senior widows—never claim their statutory entitlements because rules are scattered across hundreds of dense circulars, application procedures are opaque, and touts charge illegal bribes.

Hakku (ಹಕ್ಕು in Kannada, हक़ in Hindi) means Statutory Right. Our mission is to compute every rupee the Republic legally owes a citizen in under 90 seconds, producing an authoritative printable A4 voucher that counter operators cannot reject.

2. Engineering Architecture: Deterministic Rules Engine

Unlike generic AI chatbots that frequently hallucinate non-existent government grants or dead links, Hakku employs a deterministic Boolean matching oracle (matcher.js). Eligibility gates (age, income ceiling, occupation, caste category, landholding, BPL/AAY card, state jurisdiction) are verified mathematically. When multiple overlapping schemes conflict, our statutory anti-double-dipping resolver automatically chooses the option maximizing citizen payout.

3. Privacy by Design: DPDP Act 2023 Safe Harbor

Hakku stores zero citizen personal data. The entire pipeline executes in ephemeral, volatile serverless RAM. All request and response envelopes are encrypted in-flight using ECDH P-256 and AES-256-GCM. When you close your browser tab or click "Erase Session", all data evaporates instantly.

4. Multilingual & Indic-First

Hakku is built natively for India's linguistic diversity, offering 100% full application translation across Kannada, Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, and English with zero English fallback leaks. Screen readers, voice dictation, and speech synthesis enable access for every citizen.
