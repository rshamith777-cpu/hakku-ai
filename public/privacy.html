Privacy Policy & DPDP Alignment

LAST UPDATED: NOVEMBER 2026 · VERSION 5.5

1. Zero-Database Architecture (DPDP Act 2023 Section 6)

Hakku is engineered from the ground up to respect citizen privacy. Unlike standard commercial portals, Hakku operates with zero persistent database storage. We maintain no SQL, NoSQL, Redis, or cloud storage buckets for citizen intake data. Citizen profiles (age, gender, income, category, landholding, ration status) exist strictly in transient, volatile serverless RAM during eligibility matching and are destroyed upon process termination.

4. Third-Party Services: BharatCode AI & QR Server Disclosures

Hakku minimizes all external network requests. Two external services are utilized under strictly defined conditions:

  • BharatCode Model API (api.bharatcode.ai): When you use the natural-language situation box (or voice dictation) and a valid API key is configured, Hakku sends only the text prompt you typed (e.g., "I am a 45-year-old farmer with 2 acres") to extract structured entity dials. Zero demographic form dials, zero financial figures, zero IP addresses, and zero session tokens are transmitted to BharatCode. If no key is set or the prompt is empty, no network request occurs and processing falls back to 100% local regex rules. Never enter Aadhaar, PAN, or bank account numbers into free-text fields.
  • QR Server Service (api.qrserver.com): To allow citizens and cyber-café operators to scan official government portal links on mobile phones or printed slips, QR code images are rendered via api.qrserver.com. The query parameter sent to QR Server contains strictly the public official government URL (e.g., https://pmkisan.gov.in). No personal data or citizen attributes are ever sent. Because your browser fetches this image directly, QR Server may receive standard HTTP client connection metadata (your public IP address and browser User-Agent) under standard HTTP operation.

5. Ephemeral Session Keys, Expiration & Instance-Local Erasure

Transmission is secured via ECDH P-256 with HKDF-SHA256 and AES-256-GCM envelope encryption. Session tickets expire automatically after 20 minutes (1,200,000 ms). Clicking "Erase Session Data Now" immediately destroys your session keys and tickets in browser memory. On the serverless backend, the ticket identifier is recorded in an in-memory revocation map; because Netlify executes in multi-instance serverless containers, server-side revocation is instance-local, and primary privacy protection is guaranteed by client-side key zeroization and the short 20-minute expiry.

6. No Tracking Cookies or Third-Party Cross-Site Profiling

Hakku uses no advertising trackers, no cross-site tracking cookies, and no third-party profiling scripts. Local browser state is held strictly in volatile window object memory and ceases to exist when you close or reload the browser tab.

7. Official Government Portals & External Links

All application deep-links point exclusively to official, verified Indian government domains (.gov.in, .nic.in). When you navigate to an official portal (e.g., Seva Sindhu, National Scholarship Portal, PM-KISAN), your interaction is governed by that government department's privacy policy.

8. Contact & Open-Source Audits

For privacy inquiries, technical audits, or vulnerability reporting, reach out via our Contact Page or submit an issue on our official open-source repository.
